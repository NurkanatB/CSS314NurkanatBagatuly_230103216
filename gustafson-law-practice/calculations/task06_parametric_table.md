# Task 06 — Parametric Speedup Table (α = 0.04)

Formulas: S(P) = P − α(P − 1), E(P) = S(P)/P = 1 − α(P − 1)/P. Generated by
`python benchmarks/generate_calculations.py` (float64; values shown to 4–6 decimals).
These are **theoretical predictions**, not measurements.

| P | S(P) | E(P) | E(P) % | Lost processors P − S |

| 16,384 | 15728.6800 | 0.960002 | 96.000% | 655.32 |

Worked example, P = 1024: S = 1024 − 0.04·1023 = 1024 − 40.92 = **983.08**; E = 983.08/1024 = 0.96004.

## Interpretation
* Speedup grows essentially linearly, with slope 1 − α = 0.96: every added processor contributes 0.96 of a processor.
* Efficiency does **not** collapse as P grows exponentially: it decreases monotonically and saturates at 1 − α = 0.96
  (E(16) = 0.9625 → E(16384) = 0.96002). The absolute loss P − S = α(P − 1) grows linearly, but proportionally it is constant.
* The "degradation" is therefore tiny: 2.5 percentage points over three orders of magnitude of P.
  This relies on the assumption that α stays 0.04 as P grows (see Task 12 for why this is optimistic).
